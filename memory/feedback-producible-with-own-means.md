A constraint in the brief is a design input, not a problem to buy away. "We have no place inside" → every
concept is outdoors in free, legally accessible public places. Never add pole studios, tattoo studios, rentals
or venues the photographer would have to book unless they ask for one. Rigs come from `references/gear.md`.
For outdoor shoots go **location-first**: scout ~20 places with character, pick ~10, then write a story per place.
**Use the location as found.** No set dressing and no props they have to hunt down (tablecloths, candle holders,
silverware, petals): the place's own furniture, mess and light are the set; wardrobe + their lights + haze only.
At most one small pocketable prop when the image truly needs it. Describe set pieces exactly as the real photos
show them, or the image model invents a bigger, better set.
**Why:** a round needed a pole studio, a tattoo studio and two Airbnbs: "We said we have no places inside, so
focus on outside shots." Two banquet concepts in an abandoned hall: "we aren't going to redecorate the hall or
bring all those props that I don't have time to find... scrap those".
**How to apply:** new-shoot (brief), scout-locations, develop-concepts self-critique.
