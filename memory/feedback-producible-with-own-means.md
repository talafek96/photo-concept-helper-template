A constraint in the brief is a design input, not a problem to buy away. "We have no place inside" → every
concept is outdoors in free, legally accessible public places. Never add pole studios, tattoo studios, rentals
or venues the photographer would have to book unless they ask for one. Rigs must come from
`references/gear.md` plus cheap portable props; nothing they don't own.
For outdoor shoots go **location-first**: scout ~20 places with character, pick ~10, then write a story per place.
**Why:** a round of concepts needed a pole studio, a tattoo studio and two Airbnbs for a team with no indoor
space and no pole: "We said we have no places inside, so focus on outside shots."
**How to apply:** new-shoot (brief), scout-locations, develop-concepts self-critique.
