Every location on a board must match how it looks **today**. Verify with recent Google user photos (internal)
and Commons photos ≤ ~3 years old (published, with year in the caption). AI frames get `loc_refs` of the real place.
**Why:** a historic street was shown from a ~2010 "pastel row" photo; it's now renovated cream with towers
behind, and the photographer noticed. Earlier generic AI help had turned a real city into a fake foreign one.
**How to apply:** before describing or rendering any real place. Sensitive/changed sites (memorials) are dropped.
