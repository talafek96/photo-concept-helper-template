When the photographer asks for bold: bolder styling with more skin (latex, harness, bodice, slip, lingerie or
swimwear as outerwear, wet look, fishnets, bare legs), confrontational expressions, hard/coloured light. Sheer
always over opaque; no nudity/explicit content. Don't announce it on the board; just put such ideas in.
Range requests ("bold, less bold, spicy, less spicy"): label each concept soft · editorial · bold · spicy.
Spicy = lingerie/boudoir/wet-look/swimwear, sensual poses, within what the model already publishes.
**Ask, don't guess, after two misses:** offer concrete options (sensual/more skin · edgy/provocative ·
surreal · story-driven; strong single image vs story) and save the answer here. One photographer's answer:
bold = SENSUAL + EDGY/PROVOCATIVE (rule-breaking in public, gritty, a bit dangerous-looking), one strong visual,
light story; surreal gimmicks and clever prop plots read as boring.
**Why:** a sidewalk-haircut "bold" concept: "I meant something that shows more skin"; two full boards rejected
before asking. The photographer's limits (`user-photographer.md` → Boldness) cap this scale.
**How to apply:** bold requests; Google refusals → OpenRouter, then fal (tell the photographer it's the weaker model).
