When the photographer asks for bold: bolder styling with more skin (latex, harness, bodice, slip, body paint,
bare legs), confrontational expressions, hard/coloured light. Sheer always over opaque; no nudity/explicit
content. Don't announce it on the board; just put such creative ideas in.
**Why:** a sidewalk-haircut concept was once offered as "bold"; the photographer meant something that shows
more skin, not a stunt. Ideas called "nice but not bold" lacked bolder outfits.
The photographer may ask for the whole range ("bold, less bold, spicy, less spicy"): label each concept on a
scale (soft · editorial · bold · spicy). Spicy = lingerie/boudoir/wet-look/swimwear, sensual poses, within
what the model already publishes; still no nudity, no explicit acts, coherent tasteful images.
The photographer's own limits (`user-photographer.md` → Boldness) cap this scale.
**How to apply:** bold requests; route Google refusals to fal (`--provider fal`) rather than toning down.
