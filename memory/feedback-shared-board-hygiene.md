The board goes to the producer and the model as-is. No questions to the photographer, no "Questions for
<model>" section, no TBDs; uncertainties become production notes with an action. Written in the board
language from `user-photographer.md` (or the brief), English terms only where natural.
Real photos labelled with credit + year; AI frames labelled as sketches.
**Why:** "Don't ask questions directed to me in the document or leave any non-finalized comments there - that
document will be shared with them." A "Questions for the model" section had to be removed.
**How to apply:** every board publish; ask the photographer things in chat, never on the board.
