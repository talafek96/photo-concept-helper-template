Check props, plants, weather and daylight against the shoot date (`references/seasonality-<region>.md`; create
or extend it for the photographer's region when you verify something).
**Why:** "make sure that anything seasonal you suggest to me is actually something that's in season now."
Oranges (a grove concept, an orange-peeling frame) were out of season for the shoot month and had to be replaced.
**How to apply:** concept design, frame text and board copy; extend the seasonality reference when you verify.
