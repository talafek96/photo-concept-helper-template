When the photographer wants striking portfolio images (not a narrative), use this format:
1. **Place first, chosen for visual character**, scouted in parallel by area (a tiny old petrol station,
   mirror-steel sculptures, a lifeguard tower at night, a civic fountain pool, a vaulted alley, a 24/7
   laundromat, a shuttered market, surf on boulders), near the model, each with the hour it is empty.
2. **One killer image per concept** that works without a caption, ~5 frames around it, at most ONE twist.
   No prop-plots, no surreal gimmicks, no cute gags, no 6-10 frame "films".
3. **Boldness as the photographer defines it** ([[feedback-bold-means]]): e.g. the model's own statement pieces
   worn as outerwear in a public place at a private hour, rule-bending, hard flash, one gel, direct gaze.
4. **Light physics written into each concept's `look`** before generating (what each source hits, the colour
   of the shadow, where haze is), as effects from unseen sources, never gear names.
5. **Frames audited by a fresh agent** (identity, tattoo placement, wardrobe, opacity, real architecture,
   physics) and regenerated until clean; cut a frame rather than show a wrong one.
**Why:** five boards of "boring", "don't make sense", "not the bold kind I asked for", then "This is MUCH
better!" The turn came from asking the photographer what bold meant instead of guessing a third time.
**How to apply:** `develop-concepts` default format; [[feedback-boring-test]].
