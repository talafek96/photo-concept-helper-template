Before scouting, find out where the model lives, how she gets to the shoot and what weekday it is. Centre
every location on the least mobile person (~30 min around her), not on the photographer's home base; if the
photographer drives, they can pick her up. Places on the photographer's route are stretch options only.
**Weekly closures / rest days** (e.g. Shabbat in Israel: no buses/trains; markets, malls, flea markets closed
or packed; closed business/industrial streets are empty and good): for every location note its status on the
shoot weekday (open/closed/crowds) and parking.
**Why:** scouting once started around the photographer's city while the model, with no car and a no-transit
shoot day, lived an hour away: "You can't give ideas that are far away from her."
**How to apply:** new-shoot intake, scout-locations, every location card on a board.
