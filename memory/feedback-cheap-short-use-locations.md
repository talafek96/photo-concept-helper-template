The photographer needs places to shoot in for hours: free public spots first (and ONLY those when the brief
says there's no indoor space, see [[feedback-producible-with-own-means]]); paid interiors only when asked:
Airbnb day-use / one night, boutique hotels, then shoot-location sites. Never long-term rental or sale sites.
State the price; check it against the budget habits in `user-photographer.md` (the default assumption: a few
hundred in local currency for a session is fine, ~4× that for 4h is too much).
Before declaring "no match", widen the radius and loosen to "fixable with gel/grade/props".
**Why:** scraping long-term rentals got "I'm not going to RENT AN APARTMENT"; a shoot-location flat at a
high hourly rate got "way too expensive... give me more options". A wider Airbnb search found real matches.
**How to apply:** any interior/venue search. Confirm the channel in one line before a large scrape.
