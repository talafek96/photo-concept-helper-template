Before any image, write each concept as ONE line: *the place + the killer image + (at most) one twist*. If
it's "model does her thing at a nice place", kill it. Kill any concept that repeats a genre or setting the model
already posts a lot (her yoga, her pole, her own room): give her something NEW built on her personality.
Her owned **statement pieces** (boots, a lingerie set, a wig, gloves) are NOT banned: put them in the new
stories. Several concepts must be unusual to the eye (an image they haven't seen: giant shadows, objects out of
place, mirrors); they go at the TOP of the board. Ask "would the photographer stop scrolling?" honestly;
replace, don't polish, a dull concept.
**Why:** boards rejected as boring (yoga at sunrise, pole in a studio, her own set in her own colours), then
"you didn't use her boots or lingerie at all... why?" and "more unusual and interesting to the eye... on the
top of the board".
**How to apply:** develop-concepts step "boring test" before writing JSON; list the killed ideas in notes.md.
