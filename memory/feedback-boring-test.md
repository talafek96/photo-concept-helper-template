Before any image, write each concept as ONE line: *who she is in the story + what she wants + the turn that
surprises + the payoff*. If the line has no turn, or is "model does her thing at a nice place", kill it.
Kill any concept that repeats a genre the model already posts a lot (her yoga, her pole, her own lingerie
set in her own colours): the shoot must give her something NEW, built on her personality, not her content
categories. Ask "would the photographer stop scrolling?" honestly; replace, don't polish, a dull concept.
**Why:** a whole board (two rounds) was rejected as boring: yoga at sunrise, pole in a studio, the model's
own lingerie set in a laundromat. "Everything you gave me was boring."
**How to apply:** develop-concepts step "boring test" before writing JSON; list the killed ideas in notes.md.
