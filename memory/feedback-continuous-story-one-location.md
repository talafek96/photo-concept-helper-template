Beautiful unrelated photos are NOT a concept. One location (or one compound/tiny walking radius), one
character, cause → effect → climax → ending, readable from the images alone. Variety from action, angle, light,
props and emotion, not new places or outfits.
**Why:** the biggest frustration with earlier generic AI suggestions ("Port City Drift", "In-Between Room"):
mood boards of unrelated pretty places.
**How to apply:** every concept and every remake; self-critique the middle frames for interchangeable poses.
