# Verified Israeli locations, venues and permits

> **Example region file.** This came with the template: it was built while scouting real shoots in Israel
> (Haifa, the north, Tel Aviv-Yafo, the coast). Use it as is if the photographer works in Israel, and as a
> model of the level of detail otherwise. If they're based elsewhere, `onboard` creates
> `references/<region>-locations.md` (same structure, starting empty) and scouting fills it as places get
> verified. The skills read `references/<region>-locations.md` for the photographer's region.

Everything here was checked in Oct-Nov 2026 (photos opened, pages read). Places change: re-verify with
recent dated photos before putting one on a board, and update the date here when you do. Ordered by area,
not by anyone's home base: always centre the radius on the least mobile person of the shoot.

## Permits and contacts
- **Haifa**: filming permits through the municipality's filming office; apply ~3 weeks ahead.
- **Tel Aviv-Yafo**: apply in advance to the municipality for commercial shooting in public space (small
  crew, no blocked passage, no gear in the road is the easiest request). Exact rules not confirmed.
- **Ein Hod**: coordinate with the village management. **Zichron Ya'akov**: local council.
  **Suzanne Dellal plaza**: coordinate with the centre.

## Where to search for interiors (in this order)
1. Airbnb (day use / one night; message the host about photography + water/props). `scripts/listings/`.
2. LocationZ (locationz.co.il, ~500 shoot locations by the hour; mostly centre; pricey) + their free scout
   service.
3. Booking (boutique hotels in old buildings: Akko, Jaffa, Haifa downtown).
4. Not: long-term rental or sale sites (Yad2 etc.). A shoot needs a few hours, not an apartment.
