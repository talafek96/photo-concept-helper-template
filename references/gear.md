## Patterns that worked (append as boards get approved)
Generic starting patterns, learned on earlier shoots with a strobe + speedlight kit. Keep the ones this kit can do.
- **Direct on-camera flash, film-snapshot look:** hard speculars on wet skin/water, fast falloff, warm-green
  cast, grain. Rear-curtain sync to freeze water drops with motion trails.
- **Single-gel concepts** (one colour at a time when asked): green, red (giant shadow on a concrete wall +
  fan), blue (backlight down a hallway + feathers), amber, cyan. The colour should mean something in the story.
- **Black-studio lacquer look:** the strong light + amber gel + grid high at 45°, a black flag so nothing hits
  the black wall 3 m behind, a second light with amber rim from behind; 70-200 at f/4, 1/200. Visible beams
  only with haze.
- **Blue-hour street:** drag the shutter for car light trails, a gridded strobe to pop the model off the
  ambient, 1/4 CTO to match street lamps.
- Lighting text must be physically honest: where each light stands, what it hits, what the wall does, why
  the shadow falls where it does. When it's not obvious, draw it (`lighting-diagram` skill).
