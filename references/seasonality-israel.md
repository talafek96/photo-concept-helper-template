# Seasonality in Israel (check against the actual shoot date every time)

> **Example region file.** It came with the template, verified for Israel in November 2026. If the
> photographer works elsewhere, `onboard` creates `references/seasonality-<region>.md` with the same table
> structure (starting empty) and every verified item gets added there. Skills read
> `references/seasonality-<region>.md` for the photographer's region.

Verified while planning November 2026; extend this file whenever you verify a new month or item.

| Item | In season | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Persimmons (Sharon fruit) | Oct-Dec | Peak November. Glossy orange with green calyx; sold with leaves at markets. |
| Pomegranates | Sept-Nov | Commercial season can run to end of November, but orchards were not confirmed for November: book early in the month and phone the farm the week before. |
| Clementines / mandarins | from mid-December | NOT November. |
| Oranges (citrus groves) | winter | Not in season in November; old irrigation-pool groves in the north weren't found visitable (Pardes Minkov, Rehovot is a preserved one). |
| Bougainvillea | flowers into autumn | Shedding late autumn: it suits "falling blossoms" stories. |
| Oleander | summer-autumn | Line many Tel Aviv streets. |
| First rains | November | "First rain" stories fit November; also means weather backup days. |

## Light and daylight (coastal Israel, November)
- Sunset ≈ 16:40-16:50 (IST, after the clocks change in late October); blue hour ≈ 16:50-17:15.
- Golden hour roughly 15:40-16:40. Mornings: sunrise ≈ 06:05-06:25; empty streets until ~08:00.
- Plan public-street shoots at early morning (empty, shops closed) or blue hour.

## Rules
- A prop or plant that isn't in season on the shoot date doesn't go on the board, even in the AI frames
  (an orange-peeling frame was replaced by a heel-on-again frame for this reason).
- If unsure, say what to check and with whom, in the production notes (not as a question on the board).
