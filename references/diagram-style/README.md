# Diagram style references

Drop 2-3 lighting diagrams in the style you want every diagram drawn in (`scripts/diagram3d.py` attaches up to
three images from this folder, alphabetically). Use images you have the right to use; keep anything you may not
share out of git (add the filenames to `.gitignore`).

With no images here, `diagram3d.py` uses its built-in style text: cream paper, black ink line-art scene in
perspective, people as line-art with light colour washes, realistic grey gear on stands, translucent coloured
beams from each modifier to what it lights, bold uppercase labels with dashed arrows. A photographer picked
that look from a friend's hand-drawn diagrams and asked for it on every diagram.
