# Reviewing a frame's images (photo + POV diagram + overview)

Put all images of the frame **side by side** (`python3 scripts/sheet.py` or a quick PIL montage) and check
them against each other, not one at a time. Every item below is a mistake that reached the photographer.

| Check | What went wrong before |
|---|---|
| **Pose and props agree across all images** | POV showed him lunging on one knee with her across his thigh; photo and overview had both knees down, her on the ground. |
| **Walls match the real place and the photo** | One flat back wall in the diagram; the real courtyard (and the photo) is a corner with a wall coming forward on the right. |
| **Every stand stands on real ground** | A rim light "behind her" on a bridge where behind her was a railing and air. |
| **No light or stand inside the lens frame** (the blockout's FRAME CHECK, then look) | A softbox visible in the photo corner. |
| **Light direction matches the reference** | Key at camera-left while the painting is lit from above, upper right. |
| **Beams start at the modifier and END on what they light** | Beam drawn over their heads; a flash head facing the viewer. |
| **Modifier shapes match the real gear** (`references/gear.md`, `references/gear-photos/`) | A square softbox instead of a 90 cm octa; a mains cable on a battery fogger. |
| **Physics in the photo** | Haze lost after an edit; red shadows on a white wall; light patches with no source. |
| **Front/behind is readable** | In an eye-level view the photographer couldn't tell the backlight was behind them, so use the elevated overview. |
| **Camera height matches the reference** | The painting was shot from the ground; the first version was at eye level. |
| **Labels** | Spelling ("almed"), each label once, exactly the gear names and numbers in the plan. |

Then the independent gaffer review (SKILL.md step 6). Report what changed, not "fixed".
